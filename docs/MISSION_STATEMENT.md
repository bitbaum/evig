## Official Mission

**evig makes access to intelligence affordable to everyone — good, durable hardware,
the software and knowledge to use it, and the AI and (in time) robotics that
increasingly decide who gets to take part. Curated, not junk refurb; a
market-and-quality model run as a non-profit, where affordable pricing is the
product, not a subsidy.**

The single sentence behind everything: **intelligence should be reachable by anyone,
anywhere — and kept reachable, for longer.**

## Origins — Revamp-IT

evig is a 2026 spin-off from [**Revamp-IT**](https://www.revamp-it.ch), the Zürich
circular-IT non-profit it grew out of. We name and honour that root — Revamp-IT's
history is **Revamp-IT's**, never claimed as evig's own.

- **Revamp-IT** — *im Handelsregister eingetragener, selbsttragender gemeinnütziger
  Verein*, Zürich. Since **2003**: refurbishing used hardware, installing Linux and
  open-source software, running workshops, and making computing accessible to people
  with limited means. Address: Birmensdorferstrasse 379, 8055 Zürich · https://revamp-it.ch
- **The divergence** — evig wanted to carry the same circular-IT conviction fast and
  far, as an independent organisation aimed at *access to intelligence* (AI, robotics,
  open tech). Revamp-IT stays with repair and reuse at a calm, local pace. A different
  goal and tempo — both legitimate. So evig set out on its own: full ownership, no
  committee to convince, the same roots aimed at a bolder destination.

## Core Principles

### 1. Access to intelligence is the inequality worth closing

Inequality of income and wealth are symptoms; the inequality that compounds hardest
today is **unequal access to intelligence** — the compute, AI, and (soon) robots that
multiply what a person can do and hand back their time. evig exists to put those tools
within reach of people, not just institutions and the wealthy.

### 2. Circular economy is the affordability engine

Good technology kept in circulation instead of turning to waste is what makes it
affordable. A refurbished laptop is both the greener choice **and** the cheapest
on-ramp to AI that exists. Reuse as energy, not obligation — *this still has a life in
it; let's raise it for longer.*

### 3. Complete, working, honest — quality is a promise, not a lottery

evig sells complete, working machines (and, in time, robots) — never parts, never
"good enough." When we say a machine is good for a real purpose, the software is
installed and configured and it works the moment you open the lid. Fitness for a
concrete purpose is the product; a spec sheet isn't.

### 4. Hardware, software, and knowledge — handed over together

Owning the box isn't the point; *doing the thing* is. evig brings three things closer
to people: **hardware** (curated, capable, reachable), **software** (set up and honest
about what it's for), and **knowledge** (workshops, repair café, a technician network,
and research published in the open).

### 5. Research in the open

evig investigates — affordable AI, running intelligence on humble and second-life
hardware, open robotics, repair, sovereignty over your own tools — and publishes what
it finds. R&D is the engine under the curation, and the honest basis for every promise
evig makes. We never cite a finding we haven't actually published.

### 6. Ways to raise your way to intelligence

Alongside buying outright, evig is building pooled and shared financing so access
doesn't depend only on what's already in your wallet: subscription crowdfunding,
shared-subscription pools, and direct support (donate a device, fund a tool, sponsor a
workshop seat). The tagline lives here: **raise for longer.**

## Legal & non-profit status

evig is a **gemeinnütziger Verein in Gründung** (non-profit association being founded),
2026, Zürich. Until the Verein is registered **and** recognised as tax-exempt by the
canton, evig does **not** issue tax-deductible donation receipts and does not claim
recognised charitable status. It is stated plainly as "in Gründung", never hidden.

The platform is open-source ([MIT](../LICENSE)) so other circular-economy
organisations can adopt it directly.
