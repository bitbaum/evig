# evig — Go-to-Market, August 2026

One page. Concrete. Review monthly. (Positioning derives from
[`EVIG_STORY.md`](./EVIG_STORY.md) + [`MANIFESTO.md`](./MANIFESTO.md) — this doc

## Positioning (one breath)

**Affordable intelligence.** evig curates complete, working second-life
machines and makes them affordable — hardware, software, and knowledge handed
over together. Circular economy is *how* it's affordable; access to
intelligence is *why* it matters. Honest by default: CO₂ figures with open
methodology (`/transparenz/co2`), no invented numbers, "Verein in Gründung"
stated plainly.

## First paying customer — shortest path

The shop IS live: `/marketplace` sells refurbished stock (is_revampit listings)
and P2P listings with one checkout. **Payrexx (Karte/TWINT) is the live,
default processor** (escrow-capable — backs the P2P flow). BTCPay (Bitcoin,
CHF-denominated) is fully wired in code but **needs server provisioning**
(`BTCPAY_SERVER_URL`/`API_KEY`/`STORE_ID` unset → hidden at checkout); Taler
adapter same status. Secondary paid paths already built: workshop
registrations, membership (`/mitglied-werden`).

**Play:** publish weekly → every post links a purchasable surface → newsletter
captures the returners → first orders come from the RevampIT orbit + Mastodon
locals. No ads, no paid channels — trust compounds, spend doesn't.

## Channels (in rollout order)

1. **Blog + RSS — working now.** SEO-complete after the 2026-08 reader fixes
   (real 404s, canonical/og/JSON-LD, localized dates/categories, feed
   autodiscovery on every page). Cadence: 1 post/week, DE + EN.
2. **Newsletter — wire-up exists.** `NewsletterSignup` on the blog index →
   `/api/newsletter/subscribe` → self-hosted Listmonk (`listmonk/config.toml`).
   Monthly digest: new posts + new marketplace highlights. (Config changes out
   of scope here.)
3. **Mastodon — primary social channel.** We operate our own instance:
   **net.miaumuh.ch** (separate VM, upgrade runbook in the blog, unlisted).
   Create/confirm an `@evig` account there; cross-post every blog post
   (drafts in `content/social/queue.md`, George approves + posts manually).
   Fediverse is the natural habitat of the FOSS/circular audience — and
   self-hosted social matches the sovereignty positioning exactly.
4. **LinkedIn — secondary cross-post** for the NGO/municipal/procurement
   audience. Same queue file, same manual approval.
5. **Local Swiss channels — after 4+ weeks of cadence.** Repair-Café network,
   Zürich sustainability groups, Quartier newsletters, circular-economy
   meetups. Lead with the CO₂ methodology and the "complete machines, not
   parts" promise — both are differentiators no generic refurb shop states.

## Metrics (honest, from our own systems — no vanity dashboards)

- **Newsletter subscribers** (Listmonk subscriber count) — leading indicator.
- **RSS reach** (feed.xml requests in Caddy logs, deduped by IP/UA).
- **Shop orders** (`orders` in DB: count + CHF, split refurb vs P2P) — the
  metric that matters.
- **Blog → shop click-through** (marketplace sessions referred from /blog).
- Review monthly against the mission rule: metric-per-franc must hold
  (devices rehomed, CO₂ avoided — from the org-numbers SSOT).
